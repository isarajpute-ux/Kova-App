# Kova: notes for whoever picks this up (Claude or a person)

Kova is a health app (Today, Workout, Fuel, Sleep, Vitals, Learn) with a Work OS (tasks, meetings, automations, an AI team, Studio for posts). One `index.html` app, a Node server in `functions/`, phone apps from the same code in `native/`.

**Read `VERCEL-STEP-BY-STEP.md` to put it online, and `SETUP.md` for every key and setting.**

## Where things are
- Code: this folder (on the owner's Mac: `~/Downloads/kova-deploy 2`). A full git backup also sits in `~/Kova/backup/kova-app.bundle` (`git clone ~/Kova/backup/kova-app.bundle kova` restores everything, every branch).
- GitHub: `isarajpute-ux/Kova-App`. Work goes on the branch `kova-brief-waves-1-4`; the owner merges to `main` through a pull request. **Never push to `main`.** The owner types the GitHub token; never ask for it or store it.
- Hosting: Vercel (site + server, `vercel.json`, `api/`), Supabase (photos and videos, `functions/supa.js`), Firebase (sign-in and database). Netlify config is kept as a fallback. Free tiers.

## How the code is built
- Feature modules live in `src/p08_*.js` / `.css`. `python3 src/inject_p07.py index.html` copies them into `index.html` (as `<script id="p08-x">` / `<style id="p08-x">`). Always run it after editing a `src/` file.
- `npm run build` writes `dist/`. Modules not needed on the first screen are listed in `LAZY` in `build/site.mjs` (and their CSS in `LATE_CSS`); they load right after the first screen, all at once.
- Budgets (`node tests/e2e/perf.mjs`): first load at most 450 KB (it is at 450, so new code goes in a LAZY module), usable within 3 s on a throttled phone (2.6 s now), at most 1500 elements. Measure when the Mac is quiet; Spotlight indexing makes it look slow.

## Tests (every feature has one; keep them green)
- `node tests/e2e/runall.mjs` runs every `tests/*.js` (use `P=3` if the machine is busy; a few files time out under load, rerun them alone).
- Browser suites, one at a time: `node tests/e2e/<name>.mjs` for perf, flows, contrast, keyboard, tours, pages, signin, store, park, compare, cut, camera.

## The owner's rules (they matter)
1. Every screen works in **dark, soft light and light glass**.
2. **No clutter, nothing hidden**: no "More" button, no hiding tabs; depth goes in pop-ups behind things already on screen.
3. **Real, not for show**: a feature copied from a reference must work end to end and have a test. No made-up numbers: with no data, say so.
4. **Reference images**: give each numbered image a specific place, and say back which went where.
5. **Sell the outcome, not the product** on plan and sales screens; keep prices quiet.
6. Security: never type passwords, keys or tokens; never create accounts; deploy only with the owner's go-ahead; nothing is emailed without approval; **the repo is public, so scan for secrets before every commit**; deletions only with the owner's OK.
7. Speak plainly: the owner is not a programmer. Say what to click and where.
8. **Every update ships with a tour of what is new** (from 2026-10-21): add a release at the top of `REL` in `src/p08_whatsnew.js` (id, title, short lines, and a step for each new thing on its own screen). Returning users get a "New in Kova" card once (Later brings it back once more), new users are not interrupted, and search ("what's new") plays it any time. A new page or section also gets its own tour (`KovaScreenTour.register`, or `KovaTour.run` with `KovaTour.once` for a page that is not a screen, like the Zone 2 page). Test both like any feature (`tests/whatsnew.js`, `tests/e2e/whatsnew.mjs`).

## Design notes
- **Vitals** (`src/p08_vdock.js`): one section at a time from a swipeable dock of wide squircle tiles at the bottom; a single glow slides to the picked tile; sections slide in from the side you head to. Look from three reference images in the app's accent colour: "Label: Value" titles, graphite cards with a soft colour glow, huge thin numbers, a track with a glowing ring, Steadiest (vivid tile) and Most off (dark tile). **No dot grid on Vitals** (that is for Work OS).
- **Guided tours** (`src/p07_ui.js` KovaTour): Skip, Back, Next; keys left/right/Esc. Modules add their own with `KovaScreenTour.register(id, name, steps)`. Tours exist for Today, Workout, Vitals (walks the dock), Sleep, Fuel, Work OS, Automations, Meetings and a Meeting.
  - p28: with your own data (live mode) Today, Vitals, Sleep and Workout are plain cards (KovaLive), so their tours use `KovaScreenTour.LIVE` lists; stops hidden for good are dropped before a tour starts. The light only moves with transform (smooth on phones). Test tours in live mode on iPhone WebKit too (`node tests/e2e/tours.mjs` does).
- **Your wearable** (`src/p08_wearables.js`): the Watch screen draws the device that sent the data (Apple Watch, Ultra, Pixel, Galaxy, Garmin, Fitbit, WHOOP, Oura, Ultrahuman, Galaxy Ring, Polar, COROS, Suunto, Amazfit, Withings); tap it to choose; each says honestly how its data reaches Kova.
- **Vitals taps** (`src/p08_vtaps.js`): every Vitals card opens something real (recovery breakdown, the reading, Ask Cora with the question pre-written, habit reminders).
- **Upright** (`src/p08_upright.js`): portrait lock where possible; a "Turn your phone upright" screen on a sideways phone.
- **One goal, everywhere** (`src/p08_goal.js`, p29): the Workout quiz goal steers every pillar. Workout has a "Built around" card (Train / Eat / Sleep for that goal; for swimming, running, hiking or cycling the week written out from `KovaSport`, each session opening to warm-up, drills, main set and cool-down with the words explained, "How to get faster" guides, and the test number that sets every pace). Machine scan and Form Check take sets, reps and rest from the goal, say why a machine matters for the sport, and "Add to my program" really adds it (`KovaGoal.addToPlan`). Fuel and Sleep each show what the goal asks, from your weight and your own nights. The scan also knows the stationary bike, rowing machine, stair climber and elliptical.
- **Kova's AI for photos and video** (`POST /api/ai/ask`, p28/p29): signed in (not a guest) with no key of your own, every photo reader and Form Check's technique review go through the server's key; photos are shrunk first (`KovaModel.fit`) and labelled with their real type (`KovaModel.mime`). Tests: `tests/aiask.js`, `tests/e2e/formai.mjs` (records a real clip in the browser).
- **Work OS reads what you put in** (`src/p08_look.js`, p29): Studio's "Check my photo or clip" sends 4 stills to Kova's AI (opening, framing, light, on-screen text, each app, a caption you can use); "Write it with Kova" and Maya's caption send the same stills (`/write` and `/crew/caption` take `images`). Compose's versions are written by Kova's AI when signed in (templates first, kept if the AI fails, and it says which). Workspace "Make" reads the picked files (text, PDF, photos). Files keeps the file itself (KovaBlobs + Kova storage) and "Read it" gives a summary, key points and to-dos that become tasks; a meeting's notes can be dictated and turned into tasks the same way. With your own data, Compose's signal cards use only real workouts. Tests: `tests/worklook.js`, `tests/e2e/worklook.mjs`.
- **Sign-in, one screen** (p30): the first launch shows the same boxed card as Settings (`src/p08_signin.js`: Sign in / Create account, Google, Skip for now) after the birthday question; the email-code screen is only the fallback when Firebase cannot load. Google sign-in can come back to kovactive.vercel.app itself (switched off: `window.KOVA_AUTH_HERE`; on 2026-10-14 Google still refused it with redirect_uri_mismatch, so `authDomain` is kova-ee9d5.firebaseapp.com until the URI is on OAuth client 861199831440-ecaq8jll...; check with a createAuthUri lookup before switching on). `vercel.json` passes `/__/auth/*` and `/__/firebase/*` to kova-ee9d5.firebaseapp.com (Safari blocked the old hand-back, leaving people signed out). Needs `https://kovactive.vercel.app/__/auth/handler` in the Google OAuth client's redirect URIs. The home-screen app uses the full-page way, a browser tab the pop-up. `sw.js` never caches `/__/`.
- **Form Check, fast and named** (p30): `KovaMotion.analyze` plays the clip through muted at 4x and reads frames as they show (was 220 separate seeks: over a minute on a phone; now a few seconds), falling back to seeking if it will not play. On the way it keeps 4 stills; signed in, `KovaFormAI.identify` asks Kova's AI to name the exercise (from the list, or a new one with its movement pattern), so the result says "Kova identified this" with one-tap alternatives instead of a list. Uploads go 3 parts at a time with a retry, and the server checks the parts with one listing (it used to download every part again, which timed out on phone videos).
- **Plans are 2-3 sets** (p30): every exercise in every generated plan is 2-3 working sets (was up to 5); the plan says to take them close to failure and add weight once the top of the range is reached. Goal titles are the plain names (Bodybuilding, Calisthenics, Tone and appearance, Swimming, Hiking, Cycling...), and the session length reads in hours past 60 minutes.
- **More p30**: Search (`src/p08_cmdk.js`) understands everyday words ("where's my calories", "I'm tired", typos) and never says nothing was found ("Did you mean", Ask Kova); a line only becomes a task first when it reads like one (a day, !high, a to-do word); "nutrition coach" finds Sage. Each AI teammate is their own page (`empPage`: name, a message box first, their numbers, skill, jobs, job description) and the team page lists them as rows. **Fuel check-in** (`src/p08_checkin.js`): the Nutrition Coach's latest check-in, this week from your own logs, Check in now, Every evening (their daily routine). **Second question** (`src/p08_goalplus.js`): Hiking asks the kind (day hikes, backpacking, peaks, rock climbing, bouldering); Cycling asks if you race, the date and distance, and counts down by phase (base, build, peak, taper); Calisthenics skills with step ladders (handstand push-up from pike push-ups). **Body map like you** (`src/p08_bodyshape.js`): female or male, build from body fat (entered, or estimated from height, weight and age), leaning out with your weigh-ins; the figure is reshaped point by point so every muscle still taps. p30b: every figure (the default too) is slim and defined, not a bodybuilder: each leg is slimmed around its own centre (a gap between them), hips and glutes stay modest, and the knee, shin, calf and foot shapes (10-15 px outside the leg outline in the original drawing) are fitted inside it; muscle outlines are stronger (`src/p08_bodyshape.css`). `p08-media` and `p08-lift` load after the first screen (budget).
- **AI team, the rest of the Grok Bot way** (p31, `src/p08_teamplus.js`/`.css`, hooks in `src/p08_crew.js`): **Chat** (a pill in the team menu): several teammates in one conversation; `@Sage @Maya ...` asks them in turn, each seeing what the one before said; `@everyone` goes to the Chief of Staff, who delegates; no @ and Kova routes it; hand-offs show in the chat. Every answer is a real job. **Do it again** on every finished job: run again, Save as a skill (`agent.skills`), Make it a routine (its own schedule per skill; "also while Kova is closed" registers `<emp>~<skill>` with the server, `functions/crew.js`). **Attachments**: photo, video (4 stills), PDF or text with any message; the photo reaches the model as an image (`cleanCrew` allows 4, not counted as text); storage keeps only the words (`noPhotos` in `saveJobs`); the mic dictates a voice note. **Meet your team** on a team with no jobs: pick needs, Kova turns on the ready-made routines (Morning brief, Today's training, Inbox sweep, Comments roundup, Plan my week; skills `st_*`) and runs the first job; the routines stay switchable on the Routines page. **Watch a page** (Research teammate): the server reads it every 6 hours (`watch` in `functions/crew.js`, `POST /crew/watch/check` for Check now), compares it line by line with the last copy and reports only what is new and gone (lines matching what you watch for first); page copies survive a re-register. **Search** lists the team's jobs, reports, skills, routines and watches ("Team work"). **Share into Kova** (Android only; iPhone needs the App Store app): `manifest.webmanifest` `share_target`, `sw.js` keeps what was shared in the `kova-share` cache, the app offers a teammate, the chat or a task. Tests: `tests/teamplus.js`, `tests/e2e/teamplus.mjs` (three looks; `ENGINE=webkit`).
- **Exercise pictures and stretches** (p32, `src/p08_exdemo.js`/`.css`): Kova draws its own figure (nothing licensed or downloaded): a glass figure (graphite with a light edge) moves from the start to the finish of each lift, the working muscles glow in Workout's orange. - **Zone 2 running** (p33, `src/p08_zone2.js`/`.css`): your range is the one you set, else 60-70% of heart-rate reserve when the watch gives a resting heart rate, else 60-70% of max (Tanaka or the max typed into Live heart rate). A card under Workout's Training buttons: minutes measured in Zone 2 this week against the goal (150 by default), by-feel minutes apart, the last run, aerobic pace now against 3+ weeks ago. The run screen (full screen, screen kept awake): heart rate from the strap against the band, what to do, a voice and a buzz after 10 s out of the band, time in zone, GPS distance and pace (fixes worse than 30 m, jumps over 8 m/s and standstill noise are dropped); with no strap it is a talk-test run and never counts as measured.
