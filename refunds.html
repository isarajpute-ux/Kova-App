The short version. Cancel before a free trial ends and you are never charged. If you were charged and did not mean to be, ask within 14 days and that charge is refunded. Purchases through Apple or Google are refunded by them.

Free trials

Cancel any time before the trial ends and you pay nothing. Settings → Manage or cancel subscription opens the right page in one tap.

Bought through the App Store (iPhone, iPad)

Apple handles payments and refunds for App Store purchases. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund App Store purchases ourselves, but we are glad to help you with the request.

Bought through Google Play (Android)

Within 48 hours of a charge you can ask Google directly from your Play account's order history. After that, write to us and we will refund through Google where this policy allows.

Bought on the website (Stripe)

  • 14 days, no questions. If you were charged for a new subscription or a renewal and do not want it, write within 14 days of that charge and we refund it in full.
  • After 14 days, cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep Pro until the end of the period already paid for. We do not refund part-used periods, except where the law requires it.
  • If Kova Pro did not work as described, write to us at any time and we will put it right or refund you.
  • EU and UK consumers have a legal 14-day right to withdraw from a new subscription; the 14 days above already cover it.
