The short version. Kova keeps your data on your phone first. If you sign in, a copy syncs to your own private space so it follows you to other devices. Health data is only used to show you your own numbers. It is never sold, never used for ads, and never sent to analytics. You can download everything, or delete your account and all of it, from Settings at any time.

Kova asks only for what a feature needs, and only when you use that feature. You do not have to give your name, birth date or location.

Where it is kept

  • On your device first. Kova works offline and your phone holds the main copy.
  • Your synced copy (if you sign in) is kept in Google Firebase, in a space only your account can read. The database rules block every other account.
  • Photos and videos you upload are kept in Netlify's file storage, under your account, reachable only through signed links.
  • Connected-account tokens are kept on the Kova server only, encrypted, and never sent to your device or anyone else.
  • These services may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

How long it is kept

  • Your data: until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account removes your synced data, files, tokens and reminders within 30 days, including backups.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes. Friends-feed posts: your last 60. Loud-noise spells: 14 days on your device.
  • Analytics (if allowed): kept by Google for 14 months, then deleted.

Who else processes it

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, your synced data, push notifications and, if you allow it, usage analytics (Google Analytics).
  • Netlify: hosts the app, the Kova server and your uploaded files.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when you use the chat, the AI team, meal photos or the wellness check, the question and the data it needs are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this is not used to train their models.
  • Open Food Facts (product barcodes) and USDA FoodData Central (food search): only the barcode or search words.
  • Resend: sends the sign-in code email.
  • Google Fonts: the app's typefaces load from Google's servers, which see your IP address.
  • The accounts you connect (Google, Meta, TikTok and others): only what you ask Kova to post or read.
  • Payments: Apple, Google or Stripe handle payment through RevenueCat. Kova never sees your card.

Each of these acts on Kova's instructions under a data processing agreement, except the platforms you connect, which you use under their own terms.

What Kova never does

  • Sell your data, or share it for advertising.
  • Use health data for ads or marketing, or send it to analytics.
  • Write to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Record sound. The noise meter keeps numbers only.
  • Diagnose. Kova is not a medical device and its cycle predictions are not birth control; it points you to a clinician when something looks off.

Your rights and choices

  • See and take your data: Settings → Data & privacy → Export my data downloads everything, from your phone and the server, as one file.
  • Delete: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete my account removes your account, your synced data, your files, your connected accounts and your reminders, then clears the phone. Cycle data has its own delete button in Vitals.
  • Correct: change anything you entered, in the app.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Health access (in the Health app or Health Connect), analytics (Settings → Privacy choices), cycle sync, the friends feed, reminders or the microphone at any time. It does not affect what was done before.
  • Object, restrict or complain: write to us. In the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority. In California you have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using them; Kova does not sell or share personal information.

Children

Kova is for people aged 16 and over. The app asks your year of birth before you start and does not let anyone younger continue. Kova does not knowingly collect data from children; if you think a child has used it, write to us and we will delete it.

Cookies and storage
