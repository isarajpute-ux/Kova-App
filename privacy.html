Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026
The short version. Kova keeps your data on your phone first. If you sign in, a copy syncs to your own private space so it follows you to other devices. Health data is only used to show you your own numbers. It is never sold, never used for ads, and never sent to analytics. You can download everything, or delete your account and all of it, from Settings at any time.

Where it is kept

  • On your device first. Kova works offline and your phone holds the main copy.
  • Your synced copy (if you sign in) is kept in Google Firebase, in a space only your account can read. The database rules block every other account.
  • Photos and videos you upload are kept in Netlify's file storage, under your account, reachable only through signed links.
  • Connected-account tokens are kept on the Kova server only, encrypted, and never sent to your device or anyone else.

Who else processes it

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, your synced data, push notifications, and usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase).
  • Netlify: hosts the app, the Kova server and your uploaded files.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when you use the chat, the AI team or the wellness check, the question and the data it needs are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this is not used to train their models.
  • USDA FoodData Central: food searches (the search words only).
  • Resend: sends the sign-in code email.
  • The accounts you connect (Google, Meta, TikTok and others): only what you ask Kova to post or read.
  • Payments: Apple, Google or Stripe handle payment through RevenueCat. Kova never sees your card.

What Kova never does

  • Sell your data, or share it for advertising.
  • Use health data for ads or marketing, or send it to analytics.
  • Write to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Diagnose. Kova is not a medical device; it points you to a clinician when something looks off.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Data & privacy → Export my data downloads everything, from your phone and the server, as one file.
  • Delete: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete my account removes your account, your synced data, your files, your connected accounts and your reminders, then clears the phone.
  • Health access: turn it off any time in the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android).
  • Notifications: only turned on when you ask for a reminder, and off any time in Settings.

Children

Kova is not meant for anyone under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Kova tells you in the app before it takes effect.
