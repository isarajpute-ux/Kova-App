Cookies and storage

Last updated 4 October 2026
PrivacyTermsRefundsCookiesLicences
The short version. Kova stores what it needs to work on your device: your data, your settings and your sign-in. Analytics cookies are only set if you say yes, and you can change your mind in Settings → Privacy choices. There are no advertising cookies.

Needed to run Kova (always on)

WhatKindWhyHow long
kova_*, kova.* (your logs, settings, cycle and noise data)Local storage on your deviceKova works offline from these; they are your dataUntil you delete them or the app
firebaseLocalStorageDbIndexedDB (Firebase)Keeps you signed inUntil you sign out
kova-* cachesService worker cacheLets Kova open quickly and offlineReplaced with each update
kova.consentLocal storageRemembers your analytics choiceUntil you change it

These are strictly necessary for a service you asked for, so they do not need consent. None of them is used to track you.

Analytics (only if you allow it)

WhatSet byWhyHow long
_ga, _ga_<id>Google AnalyticsCounts visits and which features are used, so we know what to improveUp to 2 years
firebase-installations-database, firebase-heartbeat-databaseFirebase Analytics (IndexedDB)The same counts inside the appUp to 2 years

Analytics never receives health values: Kova lets through only a short list of plain event names (for example "workout_complete") and drops anything else.

Advertising

None. Kova does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and does not let anyone else place them.

Changing your choice

Settings → Privacy choices in the app, at any time. Your browser's settings can also clear or block cookies and storage; blocking the "needed" ones stops Kova from saving anything.
